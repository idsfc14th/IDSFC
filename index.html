About

Where design, technology and people intertwine.

Since 2014, the Graduate School of Design at NYUST has hosted IDSFC as a platform for design research and international exchange. This year, Interwoven Intelligence asks how human sensibility, computation, local knowledge and cross-disciplinary expertise can be connected, translated and woven together through design.

Theme

Why Interwoven Intelligence?

Facing AI and algorithmic development, ecological transition, the loss of local culture and global cultural convergence, design must move beyond single technology- or tool-oriented thinking and rethink the relationships among people, technology, culture and environment.

Intelligence

Intelligence here is more than AI. It spans human sensibility, computation, local knowledge and cross-disciplinary expertise, and design can expand what we mean by intelligence.

Interwoven

Interwoven stands for open connection and dialogue among different knowledge, cultures and ways of thinking, and for designers who cross disciplinary boundaries to bring diverse perspectives together.

How to submit

Submit an abstract through the online system. After acceptance, you will be asked to upload your full paper and poster file and to pay the registration fee.

Language

All abstracts, full papers and posters must be written in English, and oral presentations are delivered entirely in English.

Full paper

A4 format, limited to 4, 6, 8 or 10 pages. Accepted full papers are published in the proceedings.

Presentation and fee

Oral presentation: 10 minutes of presentation plus 5 minutes of Q&A. The registration fee is tentatively NT$2,000.

Publication

Electronic proceedings, with an ISBN to be applied for and indexing planned in Airiti Library. Award-winning papers will be recommended to the Journal of Design Study.
